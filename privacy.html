Tine & Tide

Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch

Short version

We don't collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your spots

Stands and fishing spots you save — names, coordinates, good winds and notes — are stored only on your device. The selected spot is shared with the app's own widgets and your paired Apple Watch. Nothing is sent to us.

Location

With your permission the app reads your location once to place a spot. Sun and moon times are calculated on the device. To get the weather forecast, the app sends the spot's coordinates rounded to about 1 km (two decimal places) to the U.S. National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov), which returns the forecast for that area. Tide stations are chosen on your device and predictions are requested from NOAA by station number. Those services are run by the U.S. government under their own policies; we don't receive or store the requests.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app only checks with StoreKit whether Pro is active.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting data

Delete a spot in Stands, or remove the app to erase everything.
