Tine & Tide

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch

Short version

There are no accounts and no ads, and we don't track you across other apps. The app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can see what to improve; you can turn them off in Settings. Your spots, coordinates, notes and photos are never part of them.

Usage statistics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records which screens and features are used, paywall views, trials and purchases (the product and price, never payment details), the app version, device type and language, a random app-instance ID that resets if you reinstall, the country and region derived from your IP address, and the state or province of the selected spot. It doesn't use the advertising identifier, isn't linked to your identity, and isn't used for advertising. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. Turn it off any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics.

Your spots, log and notes

Stands and fishing spots, your hunt log, marks, markers and notes are stored on your device. The selected spot is shared with the app's own widgets and your paired Apple Watch. With iCloud sync on (Settings), they are also kept in your own iCloud account so your devices match. We can't see that data. Photos stay on the device that took them.

Location

With your permission the app reads your location once to place a spot. Sun and moon times are calculated on the device. To get forecasts and map data, the app contacts public services for the area around a spot:

  • United States: the National Weather Service (coordinates rounded to about 1 km), NOAA tides (by station), USGS rivers and public land (a map area).
  • Canada: Environment and Climate Change Canada for weather, rivers and radar (a map area), Fisheries and Oceans Canada for tides (by station), and Apple's geocoder to find the province and time zone (coordinates rounded to about 1 km).
  • Maps and radar: Apple Maps, NOAA radar and OpenStreetMap for boat ramps (a map area). Apple Maps gives driving times when you ask for them.

These services run under their own policies. We don't receive or store the requests.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app checks with StoreKit whether Pro is active.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting data

Delete a spot or log entry in the app, or remove the app to erase what's on the device. Turn off iCloud sync to stop syncing; iCloud data can be managed in your Apple Account settings. Statistics are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months and aren't tied to your name or Apple Account. To ask about them, email us.
